About us

CentriCare PTY Ltd is a trailblazing, 100% black woman-owned enterprise with a proven track record spanning over 16 years.

Established in 2009, we have proudly emerged as a preferred service provider in healthcare supplies, infrastructure development, and facilities management for both the public and private sectors in South Africa.

At CentriCare PTY Ltd , excellence is not just a goal; it is our standard. Our operational processes are rooted in internationally recognized ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems, ensuring consistency, innovation, and superior outcomes across all our services.

Our comprehensive service offerings include cleaning services, hygiene services, pest control, and waste management-ensuring every aspect of your facility operates at its best.
