ABOUT US

CentriCare is a trailblazing, 100% black woman-owned enterprise with a proven track record spanning over 16 years.

Established in 2009, we have proudly emerged as a preferred service provider, in healthcare supplies, infrastructure development, facilities management, waste management services, uniform distribution, as well as furniture supply, for both the public as well as the private sectors in South Africa.

At CentriCare, excellence is not just a goal; it is our standard. Our operational processes are rooted in internationally recognized ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems, ensuring consistency, innovation, and superior outcomes across all our services.

Our Total Facilities Management Division is a trusted leader in delivering tailored solutions that prioritize comfort, cleanliness, and operational efficiency.

By redefining industry benchmarks and leveraging innovative strategies, we transform the environments we manage, driving exceptional results for our clients.

Collaboration and partnership form the cornerstone of our approach. By building strong relationships with clients, suppliers, and stakeholders, we foster mutual success and sustainability.

Moreover, as a black woman-owned business, we are deeply committed to diversity and inclusion.

We proudly create opportunities for underrepresented communities, with a focus on empowering women to excel in traditionally male-dominated industries.
